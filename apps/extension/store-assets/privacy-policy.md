# Privacy Policy — WordStreak Chrome Extension

**Last Updated:** October 2026

WordStreak ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles user information.

---

## 1. What Data We Collect
- **Selected Vocabulary & Context:** When you intentionally highlight a word and choose "Save to WordStreak", the selected text and its immediate surrounding sentence are sent to the WordStreak API to create your flashcard.
- **Authentication Tokens:** When you sign in to WordStreak, your authentication tokens (`accessToken`, `refreshToken`) are stored securely in your browser's `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Extension Preferences:** User settings such as reminder schedules, quiet hours, and badge display preferences are stored locally using `chrome.storage.sync` or `chrome.storage.local`.

---

## 2. What We DO NOT Collect
- **No Browsing History:** We do NOT record, track, log, or sell your browsing history, URLs visited, or web activity.
- **No Keystrokes or Form Data:** We do NOT monitor form inputs, passwords, or typing outside of explicit user interaction with the extension.
- **No Third-Party Analytics/Trackers:** The extension contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking scripts, or data brokers.
- **No Personal Identifiable Data Sale:** We will never sell, rent, or trade your data under any circumstances.

---

## 3. How Data Is Transferred and Stored
All data transmitted between the extension and the WordStreak servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Authentication tokens are never exposed to third-party domains.

---

## 4. Single-Purpose Compliance
In accordance with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, the WordStreak extension adheres strictly to the Single Purpose policy: assisting users in capturing English vocabulary and reviewing flashcards.
