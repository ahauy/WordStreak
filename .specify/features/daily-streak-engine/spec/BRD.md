# Business Requirements Document (BRD): Daily Streak Engine

- **Document ID**: `BRD-STREAK-001`
- **Feature Slug**: `daily-streak-engine`
- **Date**: 2026-08-20
- **Version**: 1.0

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Daily Streak Engine provides an automated, timezone-aware daily habit retention loop for WordStreak learners. By tracking consecutive calendar days of vocabulary study and rewarding active practice with real-time mascot celebrations and streak badges, the engine drives 7-day and 30-day user retention while preventing timezone boundary errors.

---

## 2. Business Goals & Objectives

- **BG-01**: Increase 7-day user retention rate by at least $20\%$.
- **BG-02**: Drive daily active study session completion across mobile and desktop.
- **BG-03**: Eliminate timezone-shift streak loss frustrations ($< 0.01\%$ support reports).
- **BG-04**: Maintain 100% Free & Open-Source commitment without paywalled streak mechanics.

---

## 3. Stakeholder Impact

- **Learners**: Experience clear visual feedback, flame rank progression, and reliable habit tracking.
- **Product & Analytics**: Gain accurate daily active learner telemetry and habit consistency metrics.
